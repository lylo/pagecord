Moving to Pagecord doesn't mean starting from scratch. If you've been writing somewhere else, we'll bring your posts across for you – including the dates they were published and the images in them.

Imports aren't self-serve yet. You send us your export file, we do the work and show you the result, and you only commit once you're happy with it.

## Where we can import from

Most people arrive from one of these:

- **WordPress** – the platform we import from most often, from a standard WXR export
- **Blogger** – from the Atom XML file under Settings → Manage blog → Back up content
- **Medium** – from the archive at Settings → Security and apps → Download your information
- **Substack** – posts *and* your subscriber list, from Settings → Exports

We can also import from **Ghost**, **Bear**, **Pika**, and **Jekyll or other static site generators**.

If your platform isn't on this list, ask anyway. As long as you can get a reasonable export out of it, we can usually work with it.

## What it costs

Verifying the import is free. We'll bring your content in and let you check it over before anything is settled. A [Premium subscription](https://pagecord.com/#pricing) is required to keep it once you've confirmed the import looks right.

## Before you start

- **Create your Pagecord blog first**, so we know where the posts are going. The free Classic plan is fine to begin with.
- **Keep your old blog live** until the import is finished. We usually need to fetch your images from it, and they'll be lost if the site has already gone.
- **Tell us about anything you don't want**, such as drafts, old announcements, or posts you'd rather quietly leave behind.

## Ready to switch?

Email [hello@pagecord.com](mailto:hello@pagecord.com?subject=Blog%20Import%20Request) with:

1. Your Pagecord blog address
2. The platform you're coming from
3. Your export file, or a link to your existing blog if you'd like a hand getting the export out

## Questions?

If you're not sure whether we can import from your platform, or you just want to know what it'll look like before you commit, ask. We're happy to take a look first.

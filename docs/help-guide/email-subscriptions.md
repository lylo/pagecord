Premium feature. Let readers subscribe to your blog and receive your posts by email.

## Enabling email subscriptions

1. Go to **Settings** → **Audience**
2. Check **Email subscriptions enabled**
3. Click **Update**

## Delivery modes

There are two ways to deliver posts to your subscribers:

### Weekly Digest (default)

Subscribers receive a roundup of your latest posts every Tuesday morning (8am in the time zone set under **Settings** → **Account**). This happens automatically – you don't need to do anything.

### Individual

You choose which posts to send, one at a time. A post can be sent once, within 14 days of its publish date. When individual mode is enabled, published posts show a banner in the post editor with a **Send to subscribers** button. After sending, the banner updates to show the delivery date and subscriber count.

Before sending for real, you can use **Send a test email** to preview the email in your own inbox first.

Posts you send individually are automatically excluded from future weekly digests, and a post that has already gone out in a digest can't be sent again, so subscribers never receive the same post twice.

Sending individual posts is covered up to 2,500 emails a month across your account. That's ten sends to a full list of 250 subscribers, and many more with a smaller list. If you reach it, the banner shows the date you can send the next post. Weekly digests and test emails don't count.

### Switching modes

To change your delivery mode, go to **Settings** → **Audience** and pick between **Weekly Digest** and **Individual** under the email subscriptions checkbox. You can switch at any time. If you switch from individual back to weekly digest, the next Tuesday digest will include any posts that haven't been sent yet.

## Email subject lines

Individual emails use the post title as the subject line, or "New post from [your blog title] – [date]" if the post has no title. Weekly digest emails always use "New posts from [your blog title]".

## Displaying the subscription form

You can show the subscription form in two places:

- **Blog header** – Appears at the top of your home page
- **Post footer** – Appears at the bottom of individual posts

Enable either or both options in the Audience settings.

You can also embed the form on any page using the `{{ email_subscription }}` [dynamic variable](https://help.pagecord.com/dynamic-variables-for-pages).

To change the line of text above the email field, fill in **Form Text** in the Audience settings. Leave it blank to keep the default, which matches your delivery mode and your blog's language.

## Viewing your subscribers

Go to **Settings** → **Audience** to see how many confirmed subscribers you have, and to download them as a CSV. The download stays available if your subscription lapses, so your list is always yours to take elsewhere.

## Subscriber limits

Premium includes up to 250 email subscribers. Weekly digests are included; sending individual posts is covered up to 2,500 emails a month, which is ten sends to a full list. If you need more subscribers, [contact support](support.md) and we'll sort out a larger allowance for an additional fee.

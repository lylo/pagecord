Most of Pagecord can be used from the keyboard.

## Hold Shift to see the keys

Hold **Shift** on any screen and a small key appears beside everything you can reach from the keyboard. Keep holding Shift and press that key to use it. Let go of Shift and the keys disappear.

Shift still types capital letters while you're writing. To see the keys from inside a text box, tap **Shift** twice quickly. They stay visible for three seconds.

## Everywhere

These keys work on every screen:

```text
Shift + P    Posts
Shift + G    Pages
Shift + C    Comments
Shift + A    Analytics
Shift + S    Settings
Shift + M    Open the blog menu
```

With the blog menu open, **V** views your blog and the number keys switch to another blog.

Where a screen has a help button, **Shift + ?** opens it.

## Posts and pages

```text
Shift + N    New post or page
Shift + U    Published
Shift + D    Drafts
Cmd/Ctrl + K Search your posts
```

## Writing a post

In the editor, these work even while you're typing:

```text
Cmd/Ctrl + S          Save the draft, or update a published post
Cmd/Ctrl + .          Open or close the post settings
Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + F  Writing mode
Esc                   Step back out
```

**Esc** steps out one layer at a time: it closes the post settings, then leaves writing mode, then leaves the text. Once you're out of the text, hold Shift to see the keys for the rest of the screen.

Publishing has no shortcut, so a post never goes live by accident.

For formatting shortcuts such as bold and italic, see [Writing posts](writing-posts.md).

## Comments

```text
Shift + W    Waiting for you
Shift + V    Approved
Shift + R    Reply to the top waiting comment
Shift + Y    Approve it
Shift + X    Delete it
```

Approving a comment moves the keys on to the next one, so you can work through the queue without the mouse.

## Analytics

```text
Shift + D    Day
Shift + O    Month
Shift + Y    Year
← and →      Previous and next period
```

## Settings

Each card on the Settings screen has its own key, shown in its corner while you hold Shift. On the About, Blog Settings and Audience forms, **Shift + U** saves your changes.
